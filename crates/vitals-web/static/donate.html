Vitals never charges a learner, in any country. Donations pay for the compute behind every free playthrough and for new clinical scenarios — and they sit in an on-chain treasury anyone can audit, not in a black box. What a donation buys is a monthly ceiling on paid inference, not a seat and not a subscription: when the month’s ceiling is reached the bay keeps running and only the patient’s voice stops until the month turns.

Two chains on this page, and only one of them is money. The fee relay figure sits on devnet, the chain this bay anchors to: devnet SOL is minted free from a public faucet and is worth nothing — read it as a fuel gauge for transaction fees, not as an asset and not as money we hold. The treasury figures are the address printed at the top of this page, and that one is on mainnet: it is real money, the QR sends real SOL, and the explorer link carries no cluster because it reads mainnet. There are two of those figures because the address holds two things: SOL sits at the address itself, and USDC sits in the token account that address owns — a separate account, printed and linked separately, which the first dollar to arrive is what creates. They are never added together. Every figure above prints the cluster it was actually read on, live, beside it — so you never have to take this paragraph’s word for which is which. And the bills a donation actually pays are model inference and hosting, billed in ordinary currency rather than in SOL or USDC.
