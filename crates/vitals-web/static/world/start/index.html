Vitals World · first time here?

Your first shift, in ten minutes.

No signup. No wallet. Works on a phone. Every patient is invented; every country is real. What you do is recorded on Solana devnet under a key your browser makes for itself — no name, no email.

1Pick a patient

The front page is a globe. A ring on a country means a patient from there is in a bed; a filled ring means somebody is treating them right now. On the right, On the ward now lists who is waiting for a stranger, with country and level.

student is the kindest first pick · press Treat her (the button says take a shift in this picture, taken before it was renamed)

2Read the chart first — the clock is not running yet

The bedside opens without starting your shift. Read the presenting complaint, look at the monitor, and check what the people before you did (the CHART strip, bottom right). Nobody stored this chart: your browser rebuilt it from every earlier shift on the chain.

"chart rebuilt from 0 anchored shifts" — you are first · "Deteriorating" — she is getting worse while you read

3Press the green button

Take the shift · treat … — from this moment the patient's head is yours until you hand over. The top bar shows shift ends in 9:xx. Nobody else can act on her while you hold her.

The chips under the buttons are the questions and orders this case knows — tap one, or type and press Enter. Look at the monitor after every order: the numbers move by a physiology engine, so the same orders give the same numbers every time.

5Hand over

Press Hand over · record this shift twice — the first press arms it, the second sends. It takes a few seconds because it is writing to the chain. "handed over. her chain is N shifts long" means it worked; the next stranger sees everything you did, in order.

not ready to record? Leave without recording throws away everything you did and frees the bed · out of time with the tab open? the page records for you at 0:00 · closed the tab? the bed frees itself in ~75 s and nothing is recorded — an anchor carries your own signature

6Your receipt

One page: what you did, in what order, how it ended, and what you scored out of 40 — each line says why it was earned or missed — with a link to the real transaction on Solana explorer. Anyone can download the tape and replay it; the same hash must come out. The URL is yours; it does not expire.

8 of 40 — a first shift · the missed items tell you what the case wanted

Worth knowing before you go in

  • Every case is provisional until our clinical advisor has read it — the label is on every page. This is practice, not medical advice.
  • Patients can die if nobody comes (one real hour = one simulated minute). It is recorded, not hidden.
  • Two people press the same patient at once: the second is told "someone holds her". Pick another.
  • StaleHead at hand-over means somebody anchored while your chart was open. Your tape is still yours; try again from the current head.
  • The 40 marks come from rules recomputed from the tape. No AI grades you.
